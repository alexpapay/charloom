Privacy and analytics

The online converter sends your image to the Charloom server for processing in memory. The converter does not save uploaded images. The original preview uses a local browser URL. For private material, run Charloom locally.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics (G-YLDL8WREEW) loads only after you choose Allow analytics on this hosted site. Google processes network and device information and may store analytics cookies to measure site usage. We do not send images, filenames or generated text to Analytics. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our configuration.

Your choice is saved in this browser's local storage. Declining keeps the converter fully functional. Local installations do not load Analytics.

Hosting

Hosting and network providers receive request information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service. This is separate from image storage and optional analytics.
